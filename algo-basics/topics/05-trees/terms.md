```
        8
       / \
      4   9
     / \
    2   6
       /
      5
```

In this binary tree 8 is the root, and 2, 5 and 9 have no children, so
they are the {{c1::leaves::a name for childless nodes}}. 4 together with
2, 6 and 5 forms the {{c2::subtree::a part of the tree}} rooted at 4. The tree's
height is {{c3::4::count the levels}}: this Deck counts levels, the nodes on
the longest root-to-leaf path (texts that count edges say one less).
